newlib 2.2.0: the notices of the parts better-smc-mixer's device images contain
================================================================================

The JieLi toolchain (clang 4.0.1 for pi32v2) builds its C library from newlib 2.2.0
(_NEWLIB_VERSION in its newlib.h). The images link these members (NOTICE, "JieLi toolchain"):

  libc.a  ctype_ (newlib/libc/ctype/ctype_.c); memcmp, strchr, strlen, strncmp
          (newlib/libc/string/); memcpy, memset, strcmp, strcpy (the archive does not name
          their sources)
  libm.a  ceil (newlib/libm/math/s_ceil.c)

The texts below are copied unchanged from newlib-2.2.0.tar.gz as sourceware.org publishes it
(SHA-512 ab9161f1...6e4be3, as in its sha512.sum): COPYING.NEWLIB's opening and its notice (1),
which applies to the files without a notice of their own (the string functions), then the
notices in ctype_.c and s_ceil.c.
